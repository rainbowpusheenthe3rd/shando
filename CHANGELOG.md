# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

- **/emerald dream**: a guided meditation of about nine minutes, into the Emerald Dream. The world's ambient sound and
  music fade out while it lasts and come straight back in combat; moving or typing it again ends it. Sounds and the
  room first, then a slow, exhale-led breath with the breathing wind and the screen's green edge, the Dream, a kind
  wish, and back to waking. Each line has a soft caption for eyes left open.
- The source now lives in this repo (it used to be mirrored from a private one).
- The "My own voice" options do nothing in the published addon: the author's recordings are personal and aren't
  included.
