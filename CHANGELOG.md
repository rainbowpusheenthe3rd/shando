# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

- **/emerald dream** (or /dreamemerald): a guided meditation of about fifteen minutes, into the Emerald Dream,
  written to be as gentle as it can be, then two minutes of frogs at the water's edge. The world's ambient sound
  and music fade out while it lasts and come straight back in combat; moving or typing it again ends it. Sounds
  and the room first, a slow, exhale-led breath with the breathing wind and the screen's green edge, the Dream,
  kind wishes, and back to waking as the frogs rise. Each line has a soft caption for eyes left open.
- The source now lives in this repo (it used to be mirrored from a private one).
- The "My own voice" options do nothing in the published addon: the author's recordings are personal and aren't
  included.
