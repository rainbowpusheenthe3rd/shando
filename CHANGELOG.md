# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

- **/emerald dream** (or /dreamemerald): about ten minutes of guided meditation into the Emerald Dream, written
  to be as gentle as it can be, then about ten minutes of frogs at the water's edge, fading slowly back to the
  game's own sound. The world's ambient sound and music fade out while it lasts and come straight back in combat;
  moving or typing it again ends it. Each line has a soft caption for eyes left open.
- Two meditations: The clearing, and The short walk (the first version's own words). Moving ends either at
  once, and she says so; a faint line under the captions says it all along. Captions fade in and out gently.
- Options: choose the meditation, or Random (each one you haven't heard first, then in turn), and Begin.
- The source now lives in this repo (it used to be mirrored from a private one).
- The "My own voice" options only appear when recordings are present; none ship with the addon.
