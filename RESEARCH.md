# The spirit guide: research behind Shan'do, Herbalore and their sounds

Recorded 6 Nov 2026, 02:30–04:00 UK, for the player it was written for, who asked to use mental health research with reproducible results,
Haidt's *The Happiness Hypothesis*, the research on disgust and divinity, family and hierarchy (nature being his
axis of the divine), and sound-effect generation, to grow the night elf guide into something that supports
immersion, meditation, reflection, repair and growth.

Four literature reviews were run (mental health practices; moral psychology plus night elf culture; sound models
plus sound and wellbeing; *The Happiness Hypothesis*). Every DOI below was checked against Crossref (or Europe PMC /
OpenAlex) on 6 Nov 2026. **No paper was read in full text.** "Abstract" means abstract only; "metadata" means
title, authors and venue only; "secondary" means the numbers came from a search summary of the paper. Evidence
levels are written as words: confirmed, supported, hypothesis, refuted.

**The overall caution.** Effects in this field shrink when tested properly. Correcting the positive-psychology
meta-analyses for small-study bias leaves about r = .10 on wellbeing and mostly nothing on depression (White et al.,
2019). Many Labs 2 found a median replication effect of d = 0.15 against originals of 0.60 (Klein et al., 2018).
So the guide promises little and invites gently. There are **no trials of voiced in-game wellbeing prompts**: every
rule about how a voice should prompt is a hypothesis carried over from other settings.

## 1. What the evidence supports, and what the guide does with it

| Practice | Best evidence | Level | In the guide |
|---|---|---|---|

| Small valued steps, milestones | Behavioural activation SMD −0.74 (Ekers et al., 2014) | confirmed (low-quality trials) | Level, form and profession milestones; strengths named at level-ups (`level5`–`7`). Reflects on what was done, never what is owed. |
| Slow, exhale-led breathing | Breathwork g −0.35 [−0.55, −0.14], 12 RCTs (Fincham et al., 2023); cyclic sighing beat mindfulness on mood (Balban et al., 2023, one RCT) | confirmed / supported | `breath` lines after 90 s still outdoors, then `windbreath`: a wind that swells on the in-breath and falls on a longer out-breath, starting at 11/min and slowing to 6/min. | Simulated nature | Positive affect 0.40 [0.22, 0.58]; stress −0.38 (Li et al., 2023) | supported | Place lines draw attention to the landscape; Herbalore's calm lines. |

| Gratitude | d 0.17, no better than active control (Davis et al., 2016) | supported, small | Light, never prescribed (skinning thanks). |

| Time played | Little to no effect on wellbeing (Vuorre et al., 2022); perceived value, not hours, predicts it (Ballou et al., 2025) | confirmed | Never moralises about hours; `deepnight` (00:00–05:00 real time, after 30 min) is care for tomorrow, not guilt. |

## 2. Disgust and divinity, family, hierarchy

- **The divinity axis.** The clean CAD mapping of disgust to divinity (Rozin et al., 1999) is refuted in its strong
  form; violations draw mixed emotions (Kollareth & Russell, 2017). Incidental disgust doesn't harden moral judgement
  after bias correction (Landy & Goodwin, 2015). Self-disgust predicted later depression in a three-wave study
  (Powell et al., 2013; review: Clarke et al., 2019) — supported.
  → **Corruption belongs to places, never to you.** Felwood is "wounded, not wicked"; `wounded2`: "The sickness is in
  the land, not in you." The sacred comes through nature, awe and elevation, never purity talk aimed at the self.
  The Highborne ruins (`place13`, `place14`) ask for pity for hunger, not disgust.
- **Family and belonging.** Need to belong (Baumeister & Leary, 1995) — supported; relationships and survival
  (Holt-Lunstad et al., 2010) — confirmed. → `kin` lines use *Ishnu-dal-dieb* and chosen family (Shandris, raised by
  Tyrande); `home` lines on returning to Darnassus.
- **Hierarchy.** Shame tracks depression more than guilt: r = .43 vs .28, 108 studies (Kim et al., 2011) —
  confirmed (association); guilt is about the act and leads to repair (Tangney et al., 2007). Defeat and entrapment
  relate to depression (Gilbert & Allan, 1998) — supported. Respect freely given predicts wellbeing (Anderson et al.,
  2015) — supported. → She speaks as *shan'do* to *thero'shan*: earned warmth, never dominance. Setbacks are framed
  as repair (*Andu-falah-dor*, let balance be restored), never as being lesser. After three deaths in 15 minutes she
  offers one way out ("the grove will wait") and then stays quiet about dying. `teacher` lines at trainers: learning
  is an honour, not a lessening.
- **Moral Foundations.** Useful as descriptions — supported; the five-factor MFQ fails measurement invariance across
  27 countries (Iurino & Saucier, 2020); the MFQ-2 is newer (Atari et al., 2023). Not used as a scoring system.

**Delivery:** these `mend` lines play only inside a ritual you chose (a quarter of dreams, a third of vigils), at
most once a day, and one is in the Children's Week pool. Never unprompted. No promise of growth from loss (see §3).
Lore: wisps as the spirits of night elf dead who became one with the forest (warcraft.wiki.gg via search). "Mother
Moon" for Elune had no authoritative source, so it isn't used.

## 3. *The Happiness Hypothesis* (Haidt, 2006): what's followed, what's dropped

Book structure from a library catalogue record, Wikipedia and Haidt's book page; **the book text itself was not
read for this record.**

| Idea | Evidence today | Level | Used? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elephant and rider | A metaphor; dual-process theory defended (Evans & Stanovich, 2013) and attacked (Melnikoff & Bargh, 2018) | hypothesis (metaphor) | Herbalore `calm9`/`calm10` speak to habit and rhythm, not willpower |
| Changing your mind | CBT g 0.79 vs control (Cuijpers et al., 2023); MBCT relapse HR 0.69 (Kuyken et al., 2016) | supported | Only gentle, non-clinical forms: pauses and noticing |
| Reciprocity | Social bonds and health (Holt-Lunstad et al., 2010) | supported | `kindness`, `kin` |
| H = S + C + V; the "40%" pie | Pie lacks an empirical basis (Brown & Rohrer, 2020); its authors withdrew it as a diagram (Sheldon & Lyubomirsky, 2021); set points do move (Lucas, 2007; Diener et al., 2006) | refuted (40%) | **Dropped.** Never promises a share of happiness in your hands, never "you'll bounce back" |
| Control matters (noise, commuting) | Uncontrollable noise costs more (Glass et al., 1971); commuting paradox (Stutzer & Frey, 2008) | supported | `/shando quieter` and `/shando nearer`; everything optional |
| Uses of adversity | Self-reported growth ≠ measured change (Frazier et al., 2009); no genuine growth on average (Mangelsdorf et al., 2019); "growth illusion" (Infurna & Jayawickreme, 2019) | refuted as a promise | **Dropped.** Hard moments get company, not a lesson |
| Felicity of virtue (strengths) | Seligman et al. (2005) gains matched by placebo in replication (Mongrain & Anselmo-Matthews, 2012); g 0.42 (Schutte & Malouff, 2019) | supported, small | Level-ups name a strength shown (patience, care, curiosity), never as therapy |
| Divinity with or without God | Elevation short-term (Pohling & Diessner, 2016); awe (Keltner & Haidt, 2003) | supported (short-term) | Places, kindness, the moon |
| Happiness comes from between | Vital engagement (Nakamura & Csikszentmihalyi, 2003); cross-level coherence is Haidt's synthesis | hypothesis | The guide's stance: love (kin), work (professions), something larger (Elune, the forest) |

## 4. How she speaks: rules, each with its source

1. **Invite, never instruct**: "if you like", "perhaps" (Ryan & Deci, 2000; Ntoumanis et al., 2021; reactance:
   Steindl et al., 2015). The old imperative favourites ("Breathe. The forest isn't going anywhere.") are kept
   because the player chose them; new lines invite.
2. **No affirmations about the self.** Positive self-statements made people with low self-esteem feel worse (Wood et
   al., 2009). Lines speak about the moment, the world and the deed.
3. **Accept feelings, don't fix them** (Ford et al., 2018; valuing happiness backfires: Mauss et al., 2011).
4. **Concrete, not abstract, reflection** (Watkins, 2008; Nolen-Hoeksema et al., 2008).
5. **Short and rare.** At least 45 s between any two lines; each line rests 30 min, then 60, 120… (Herbalore 20,
   40, 80…); each kind has its own minimum gap. Hypothesis: the right rate is unknown.
6. **Never in combat or instances**; one line per death; a single `repair` line after repeated deaths, then quiet.
7. **No claims of therapy.** Meditation can have adverse effects (Farias et al., 2020), so nothing long or guided,
   and one command turns her off.
8. **Stay in Azeroth.** The player asked (5 Nov) that no line mention the real world; care lines (`deepnight`, `longroad`)
   are phrased in-world.

**Audit of the older lines (6 Nov 2026, afternoon).** Every line from before this research was re-read against the
rules above. Changes: older imperatives now invite (rule 1; the player's chosen "Breathe. The forest isn't going anywhere."
kept); statements about the self became statements about the deed or the world (rule 2); borrowed real-world
wording removed (a fantasy-novel quotation and a meditation teacher's phrasing; rule 8), and the lake-awakening story
replaced with in-game lore (the kaldorei settled beside the Well of Eternity; *kaldorei*, children of the stars);
*Ande'thoras-ethil* now used; lore or recipe claims that weren't checked were cut (the Blue Child's origin, what mages
and alchemists make of Mageroyal and Firebloom, a Cenarius teaching that was never sourced); and near-duplicate
herb lines rewritten so each of the three is distinct. 44 lines were re-recorded; the earlier takes are archived.

**Avoided because they failed or are weak:** ego depletion (Hagger et al., 2016; Vohs et al., 2021) — refuted;
power posing on hormones and behaviour (Ranehill et al., 2015) — refuted; pen-in-mouth facial feedback (Wagenmakers
et al., 2016) — refuted, deliberate smiling small (Coles et al., 2022); binaural beats (Garcia-Argibay et al., 2019;
Ingendoh et al., 2023; Platt & Hammond, 2024) — weak, mechanism mostly unsupported, not used.

## 5. Sound

**Generation models** (checked 6 Nov 2026 against model cards, repos and papers):

Gated on Hugging Face: needs the licence accepted and a token. **Planned primary for richer beds.** |
| TangoFlux | Non-commercial checkpoints | probably | Best prompt adherence for one-shot foley. Fallback. |
| MOSS-SoundEffect v2.0 | Apache-2.0 | probably (hypothesis) | Cleanest licence, no published evaluation. |
| Woosh (Sony) | CC-BY-NC weights | not stated | Strongest in its own table; mono. |
| AudioGen medium | CC-BY-NC | needs 16 GB | Ruled out. |

Benchmarks from different papers aren't comparable (Stable Audio Open scores FD 78 in its own paper, 150 in Woosh's).

**What's built now** (tools/spirit_sfx.py): everything is synthesised with numpy, no samples and no model, so it is
entirely ours. A two-bell chime (`linger`, skill milestones), a single bell (`kindness`), a rising swell of low
fifths with a faint high shimmer (sacred places), the breathing wind, and two 60 s looping beds (inn by day, inn by
night). Beds loop because noise is shaped on the FFT grid of the whole clip and events wrap round the end. Levels
sit well below the voice (peaks 0.2–0.35) and play on the player's Ambience and SFX sliders, near the game's own
ambience (Galbrun & Ali, 2013: water about level with the background, never above).

## 6. Night elf culture used, and its sources

Lore came from search results quoting warcraft.wiki.gg, Wowhead, Engadget/Blizzard Watch "Know Your Lore" and the
Vanilla WoW archive (Wowhead and warcraft.wiki.gg disallow automated fetching; Wowpedia returned a Cloudflare
challenge). Level: supported unless noted.

- *Ishnu-alah* (good fortune to you), *Ishnu-dal-dieb* (good fortune to your family), *Elune-adore* (Elune be with
  you), *Andu-falah-dor* (let balance be restored), *Ande'thoras-ethil* (may your troubles be diminished),
  *shan'do* / *thero'shan* (honoured teacher / student), *kaldorei* (children of the stars). *Shorel'aran* is
  Thalassian, not Darnassian: not used.
- Druids' long sleep in barrow dens, minds in the Emerald Dream; Cenarius their patron; Malfurion his student.
- Moonglade, the druids' neutral sanctuary. Sentinels, led by Shandris Feathermoon, an orphan raised by Tyrande.
- The Highborne's hunger for the Well, the Sundering and exile — told with pity, not disgust.
- Felwood: corrupted forest; Emerald Sanctuary druids cleansing it; the Shadow Council at Jaedenar.
- Zone-specific Classic lore for Teldrassil, Darkshore, Ashenvale and Winterspring was **not** retrieved, so those
  place lines are sensory, not factual. Subzone names are from memory and need checking in game (hypothesis): a
  wrong name just means that line never plays.

## 7. Untested

Nothing here has run in the game yet. A mock of the WoW API drove Shan'do through a session (login, places,
stillness, forms, skills, deaths, kindness, group, trainer, inn, dusk, late night) and caught three bugs, now fixed:
a night line beating the login greeting, once-only lines lost when another line had just played, and two care lines
back to back. Event names and arguments (`LEARNED_SPELL_IN_TAB`, `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT`, `CHAT_MSG_SKILL`) are from
the modern client API and need confirming on Forever (hypothesis).
